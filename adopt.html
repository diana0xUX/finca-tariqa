What dynamic traits mean

At the end of each harvest season, we update the NFT metadata on-chain. Your token's image and attributes reflect what actually happened that year: how much fruit the tree gave, what stage of organic certification the grove reached, whether a soil carbon measurement showed improvement.

This makes older tokens more interesting — a tree held for 4 seasons carries 4 seasons of real harvest data, a higher level title, and a carbon score that reflects genuine, documented improvement.

When you sell or transfer the NFT, the new owner inherits all that history. A Season 4 "Hillside Guardian" token with a Verified carbon score is different from a new Season 1 Seedling — and the blockchain proves it.

Metadata storage: Token metadata and tree photos are stored on IPFS via Pinata, content-addressed and permanent. When Codex — IFT's decentralized storage protocol — reaches production readiness, we will migrate to Codex as the storage layer. The token URIs will update; the content stays the same.

Generative Art

1,100 trees. 1,100 unique artworks.

Every token gets its own AI-rendered portrait — generated from scratch by a state-of-the-art image model. No two look the same. Here's exactly how it works.

Each of the 1,100 trees on the land gets its own portrait. Every portrait is built from 11 visual traits — the age and shape of the tree, the colour of its canopy, the character of its trunk, how much fruit it bears, what kind, the sky, the ground, the atmosphere, a special element, the world it stands in. Those traits are assembled into a detailed text description and sent to FLUX, an AI image model, which renders a unique 1024×1024 painting for that exact combination. From nothing.

The traits are weighted by rarity — common options appear on most trees, rare and epic ones on very few. Because each image is rendered from scratch rather than assembled from pre-drawn parts, no two portraits look like copies of each other, even when they share traits.

Two Common trees can look completely different: one with a rolling hillside and a heavy fruit load, another with a Golden Noon sky and a bee swarm. The artwork is unique to each tree. So is the tree itself.

Drop Mechanics

Built like a collectable. Rooted in reality.

CryptoKitties launched in November 2017 and within four days clogged the entire Ethereum network — 180,000 users, $14 million in trading volume. The secret was breeding, time pressure, and hidden rarity that made it impossible to stop collecting. We borrowed the best mechanics and grounded them in a real farm.

⏳
Dutch Auction
Legendaries drop by auction
The 10 Legendary trees don't sell at a fixed price. Each opens at €500 and falls €10 per day until someone claims it. You decide when it's worth it to you. Miss the floor, miss the tree.
🌿
Original mechanic
Grafting — breed new tokens
Any two Season 3+ holders can "graft" their trees together. We take a real cutting from each tree, plant a new sapling on the hillside, and mint a new Grove Token for it. The sapling inherits one visible trait from each parent — and a new trait of its own. Max 100 graftings ever.
Both holders must consent · Season 3+ required · 100 graftings total · grafted tokens are untethered from the orange subscription
🔮
Hidden mechanic
Fancy Trees — secret combos
Certain combinations of artwork traits unlock a completely different illustration — we call them Fancy Trees. Storm sky + Storm-bent shape + DANA scar element = Phoenix of the Hill. We won't publish the full list. Part of the game is finding them.
Only the first holder to land a qualifying combination gets the Fancy variant artwork. Approximately 12 Fancy Trees exist.
🌑
Mystery trait
Seasonal reveals
Three traits appear as ??? at issue. They reveal at the end of your first harvest season once we have real data: exact yield in kg, first organic inspection result, and one surprise trait determined by that year's recorded weather at Puig Gros.
📦
Collection meta-game
Complete the catalogue
Collect all 3 orange varieties → earn Grove Master badge. Collect all 5 rarity tiers → Full Spectrum. Hold tokens across all 4 season frames → Year Round. Collection badges are non-transferable — only the person who assembled the set gets them.
⏱️
Transfer rule
Harvest cooldown
If you sell your token within 30 days of the harvest dispatch date, the incoming box was already addressed to you — the new owner doesn't inherit that delivery. It protects honest adopters and makes holding through harvest season meaningful.

How It Works

From hillside to wallet.

No prior crypto experience needed. If you have a MetaMask or Status wallet, you're set. If not, we'll walk you through it.

01
Choose your tier
Pick Amigo, Padrino, or Custodio. Each comes with a specific tree, oranges, and a token — your on-chain title deed to that tree. Rarity reflects what's actually there.
02
Pay in euros or crypto
Pay by PayPal in euros, or send the equivalent in ETH, USDC, or DAI to our wallet. Email us your wallet address so we know where to send the token.
03
Receive your token
We issue your tree's ERC-721 token on Linea — loaded with its real traits — and send it to your wallet. It appears in MetaMask, Status wallet, or OpenSea within minutes.
04
Watch it evolve
Each season: yield kg, organic status, and carbon score update on-chain. Waku push notifications alert you when updates go live. Renew to level up.
Built on IFT / Logos Infrastructure

The tech stack behind the tokens.

We chose the IFT (Institute for Free Technology) ecosystem — the team behind Status, Waku, Codex, and the Logos Blockchain — because their values match ours: decentralized, privacy-first, no platform lock-in. Here's exactly what we use and when.

⬡
Linea
NFT minting chain
Status Network — IFT's planned L2 — merged into Linea in April 2026. Linea is a zkEVM L2 on Ethereum: EVM-compatible, gas fees under $0.10 per mint, full ERC-721 support. This is where your token lives.
Live now
📡
Waku · Logos Messaging
Seasonal notifications
IFT's decentralized P2P messaging protocol. When we update your token's metadata each season — new yield data, organic status change, badge airdrop — a Waku message goes to your wallet address. No email, no platform. Pure peer-to-peer.
Launching with season 2
📦
Codex · IFT Storage
Tree photos + metadata
IFT's decentralized durability storage — stronger persistence guarantees than IPFS, with ZK-based auditing and economic repair incentives. Token metadata and tree photos migrate from IPFS to Codex when Codex reaches production. The content stays identical; only the storage layer changes.
Migration planned 2027
📱
Status Wallet
Recommended holder wallet
The Status app (iOS + Android) includes a self-custodial wallet that supports ERC-721 NFTs on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, and Optimism. It is built by IFT. MetaMask also works on Linea and is the default for new users. Either is fine.
Live now
🔗
IPFS · Pinata
Current metadata storage
Token metadata and tree photos are pinned on IPFS via Pinata today — content-addressed, permanent, and industry standard. Will migrate to Codex in 2027 when IFT's storage layer is production-ready.
Live now
🌐
Logos Blockchain
Future home
Nomos — now called Logos Blockchain — is IFT's purpose-built chain. Testnet is planned for late 2026, mainnet for early 2027. When it launches with smart contract support, we plan to bridge the grove tokens to Logos. The tokens will be portable.
