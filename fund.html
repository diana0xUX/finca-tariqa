Water tested
Two well water samples analysed by accredited laboratory (M-26-0146, M-26-0147). Result: safe for irrigation — pH 7.4, conductivity and salts well within citrus tolerance, Legionella not detected. Not safe to drink without treatment (nitrates 56% above EU limit, E. coli present — agricultural runoff from upslope orchards). The well is confirmed functional. The nitrates in irrigation water are effectively free nitrogen fertiliser.

What we're building toward

Three outcomes in five years.

€0.00
synthetic inputs

Full CAECV organic certification by 2028. Retroactive recognition means the three-year conversion clock may already be running — if the grove has been clean for the past three years, we could certify early. Every investment in Year 1 advances this.

1,000+
holm oak planted

Replacing fire-prone Aleppo pine with native Quercus ilex — the long-lived, fire-resistant, self-seeding oak of the western Mediterranean limestone. A functioning woodland by year 10. The forest that should have been here all along.

2028
EU carbon market opens

The soil carbon baseline we establish now becomes five years of documented improvement when the EU CRCF market goes live around 2028. A grove that starts in 2030 starts with nothing. We start now.

How the money is used

Two years. Eight milestones. One direction.

Every line item below comes directly from the farm's research report — costs sourced from IVIA, CAECV, and Valencia-based suppliers. Nothing is estimated loosely.

Year 1 — Foundation
November 2026 – October 2027
€10,000
Precision monitoring kit SPAD chlorophyll meter (detects iron chlorosis weeks before visible symptoms), two soil moisture probes, on-site weather station. The tools that make management decisions data-driven rather than guesswork. €3,500
Soil carbon baseline Five soil core samples (0–30 cm and 30–100 cm), IVIA-accredited lab analysis for total organic carbon, bulk density, pH. Without this baseline taken now, there is nothing to compare against when the EU carbon market opens. This single action, taken before any management changes, is what makes future carbon credit claims verifiable. €500
CAECV organic certification Registration fee plus retroactive conversion period recognition application (Form FPO05-157e2). If the grove has had no synthetic inputs for 3+ years — which we're verifying now — the full 3-year conversion wait may be waived. Starting the certification clock in Year 1 means organic premium pricing by 2028. €700
Cover crops & mulch Hairy vetch (Vicia villosa) and a grass mix across 2.8 ha of citrus terraces; pruning chipper and first season of mulch under all 1,100 trees. These are the first three organic practices — compatible with current GlobalG.A.P. certification, no compliance risk, immediate soil biology improvement. €600
Holm oak — Phase 1 500 containerised Quercus ilex seedlings (12–17 cm) plus individual tree shelters, planted into the first cleared zone of the pine forest in November. This is the native species the limestone hillside wants. Every tree planted now is a 700-year investment. €2,500
Habitation certificate Architect inspection and cédula de habitabilidad for the farmhouse — the prerequisite for legal residential occupation and the first step toward tourism registration. Without it, the farmhouse cannot legally host visitors overnight. €700
Ecotourism registration Declaració responsable (Proc. 16214, Decreto 4/2024) to operate guided day visits commercially — geology walks, harvest experiences, forest tours. No permit required beyond this filing. Plus first-year civil liability insurance (minimum €600K/claim required by law). €900
Admin & professional fees PTGFS forest management plan (technical drafting, required before any pine felling), agricultor activo registration with Conselleria d'Agricultura (prerequisite for Joven Agricultor €70K grant and all PAC subsidy applications), leaf mineral analysis. €600
Year 1 total €10,000
Year 2 — Growth
November 2027 – October 2028
€10,000
ERT geophysical survey Electrical Resistivity Tomography — electrodes pressed into the soil, maps water-bearing fractures at depth across the property. The 1957 geological survey identifies the lower boundary of the property as the textbook location for a borehole. The ERT finds the crack before drilling starts. Without it, borehole siting is guesswork. With it, success rate goes from chance to science. €3,000
Precision irrigation upgrade Smart irrigation controller (Netafim NMC or equivalent), tensiometer network (6 Irrometer Watermark sensors across terrace zones), pH/EC inline sensor on the irrigation line. Overwatering on limestone soil raises local pH at drip emitters — directly worsening iron lockup. This layer prevents that and converts Year 1 soil moisture data into automated decisions. €2,000
Holm oak — Phase 2 500 additional Quercus ilex seedlings plus tree shelters, planted into the second cleared zone. Year 2 also requires twice-monthly drip irrigation for Year 1 plantings — without supplemental water, survival on exposed limestone in a dry year drops to 15–30%. With it: 60–70%. €2,500
Multispectral drone survey Annual NDVI flight by Valencia-based operator (Agropixel or Hemav). Overlaid on cooperative grade data, this map shows exactly which zones of the 1,100-tree grove produce CAT1 premium fruit and which produce low-grade destrio. The single most actionable data product available for a grove of this size. €500
Organic certification Year 2 Annual CAECV maintenance fee plus GVA subsidy application (Orden 6/2024 — 80% of annual fees covered, up to €2,000/year). The subsidy application must be filed each year; Year 2 is the first full year of certified conversion. €400
Carbon & monitoring Sentinel-2 analytics subscription (EOS Crop Monitoring, €200/year) plus Year 2 soil carbon measurement — the second data point that begins to show improvement trend. The difference between the Year 1 and Year 2 baselines is what the carbon credit auditors verify. €400
Contingency & labour Tree planting and mulching is manual work on terraced limestone — no machinery can reach the upper grove. Cover crop mowing (2–3 passes/season), tree shelter inspection and replacement, unexpected soil or agronomic interventions. €1,200
Year 2 total €10,000
