Adopt a Tree

Your tree. Your oranges. Your hillside story.

We have 1,100 orange trees on an 80-million-year-old limestone hillside in Carcaixent — three varieties across a 9-month harvest season. Adopt one and we'll send you its fruit. Every adoption comes with an NFT certificate on Linea — a living token that evolves with the tree, earns badges, and levels up the longer you hold it.

About shipping: Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your country. Spain and Portugal: approx. €8–12 per box. EU: approx. €15–25 per box. We pack in recycled cardboard, no plastic. Deliveries happen within the 9-month harvest season (October–June). You choose when.

Pay with crypto
Send ETH, USDC, or DAI — get an NFT certificate back.
If you prefer to pay in crypto, you can send the equivalent amount in ETH, USDC, or DAI to our wallet and we'll record your adoption on-chain. Every crypto adopter receives an NFT certificate — a non-transferable token that proves your tree adoption, stamped with the tree's variety, approximate age, and the season. It lives in your wallet permanently, even after the season ends.

To adopt with crypto: email us first with your tier and wallet address — we'll confirm the current ETH/USDC equivalent and send the receiving address.
NFT certificate economics
What it costs to mint, honestly.
We mint on Base (Coinbase's L2 network) — gas fees are fractions of a cent, not the $20–50 you'd pay on Ethereum mainnet. The certificate is a simple ERC-721 token. No speculation, no trading — just permanent on-chain proof.
  • Contract deployment (one-time) ~$5–15
  • Gas per mint (Base network) <$0.01
  • Platform: Manifold / Zora Free to deploy
  • Adoption price (unchanged) €99 / €175 / €290
  • Extra cost for NFT certificate None — included
  • Secondary market resale Possible (OpenSea, etc.)

Digital Certificate · Coming 2027

Invest in verified ecological land.

We're exploring a new kind of ownership — a blockchain certificate backed by the verified ecological health of this hillside. Part land story, part carbon credit, part stake in a living place. The mechanics are still being designed. Sign up and we'll tell you first.

🌍
Verified impact
Each certificate will be backed by soil carbon data, organic certification, and on-chain biodiversity monitoring — not just a promise.
📈
Grows with the land
As the holm oak forest matures and the EU carbon market opens (around 2028), the ecological value of this land increases. The certificate will reflect that.
🔗
On-chain and tradeable
Issued on blockchain. Transferable. The mechanics are still being built — but we want it to be something real people can hold, sell, or give.
