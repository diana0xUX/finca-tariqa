Finca Tariqa

A farm that earns in four directions at once.

Citrus, eco-tourism, carbon credits, and a forest in transition. Woman-owned, first-generation, post-DANA. Applying to Ma Earth Round 4, April 2027.

Four directions

What we're building.

01 Organic by 2028

Regenerative Citrus

Three-year CAECV organic conversion — registration in preparation. EU organic conversion payments of €300–600/ha/year during transition. The same practices that build soil carbon and raise terpene levels in the fruit.

02 Targeting 2026

Eco-Farm Tourism

Guided grove tours, harvest experiences, geology and forest walks — to be registered under Decreto 4/2024 (Valencian ecotourism regulation). Casa rural accommodation in the restored farmhouse. The hillside is the product.

03 Baseline 2026

Carbon Farming

Soil carbon baseline to be established with IVIA-accredited laboratory in 2026. Agreena platform for Verra VCS verified credits. EU CRCF market opens approximately 2028 — baseline started now means documented improvement ready to sell.

04 15-year project

Native Reforestation

1.7 ha Aleppo pine to Quercus ilex — PTGFS forest management plan in preparation for Q4 2026 submission. FEADER up to 85% EU co-financing — eligible to apply. In 15 years, native Mediterranean limestone woodland.

The People Behind It

First-generation. Post-DANA. A hillside nobody was watching.

Finca Tariqa was purchased in November 2025 by a woman under 40 with no inherited farming tradition. The first season was spent reading five years of cooperative records, commissioning a geology report, and learning what an 80-million-year-old hillside was trying to say.

The farm is registered with Coop. del Camp de Vila Nova de Castelló (Exagro). Organic certification through CAECV is in preparation. The forest management plan is in preparation. Eco-tourism registration is in preparation.
