Finca Tariqa

A farm that earns in five directions at once.

Citrus, eco-tourism, glamping, carbon credits, and a native forest in transition. Woman-owned, first-generation, post-DANA. Applying to Ma Earth Round 4, April 2027.

Five directions

What we're building.

01 Organic by 2028

Regenerative Citrus

Three-year CAECV organic conversion — registration in preparation. EU organic conversion payments of €300–600/ha/year during transition. The same practices that build soil carbon and raise terpene levels in the fruit.

02 Launching 2027

Eco-Farm Tourism

Guided grove walks, geology tours, harvest experiences, and overnight stays registered under Decreto 4/2024. The hillside is the product — visitors come to understand what limestone, altitude, and time do to a piece of fruit.

03 Baseline 2026

Carbon Farming

Soil carbon baseline established with IVIA-accredited laboratory in 2026. Agreena platform for Verra VCS verified credits. EU CRCF market opens approximately 2028 — baseline started now means documented improvement ready to sell.

04 15-year project

Native Reforestation

1.7 ha Aleppo pine to Quercus ilex — clearing the Franco-era plantation and replanting with the native climax species. In 15 years, Mediterranean limestone woodland and sequestered carbon. The full story →

05 Camp Tariqa · 2026–2030

Glamping, a Venue, and a Place to Stay

The long-term development of the land beyond the grove. Glamping structures on the limestone terraces, rural accommodation rooms, an outdoor event venue, and the infrastructure to host people on the hillside properly. Currently in planning and construction — the basecamp is being built by hand, this season. Full operation targeted for 2029–2030 via Declaración de Interés Comunitario.

Eco-tourism · what it looks like

The hillside is the product.

Finca Tariqa sits on an 80-million-year-old Cretaceous limestone hillside. The geology creates the terroir that makes the oranges what they are. That same story — the water, the rock, the altitude, the varieties — is what visitors come to understand.

We are not building a resort. Eco-tourism at Finca Tariqa means small groups, real work, and genuine access to the farm. A walk through the grove explaining how limestone stress concentrates flavour. A morning picking oranges in the harvest season. A night sleeping on the terrace under the Valencian sky.

The tourism offer builds in phases — from guided experiences and harvest stays in 2027, toward permanent glamping and a venue over the following years, as the Declaración de Interés Comunitario makes its way through the Valencian planning system.

2026

Building the basecamp

Volunteers and skilled builders are constructing the first infrastructure on the hillside — sleeping structures, compost toilets, a working basecamp. This is the foundation everything else rests on. The work is happening now.

2027

First guests — harvest stays and grove tours

Registered under Decreto 4/2024 agro-tourism rules. Guided geology and grove walks. Harvest weekends during the picking season. Glamping structures (demountable) in place on the terraces. Meals from the farm and local market.

2028

DIC approved — permanent structures begin

If the Declaración de Interés Comunitario is granted by the Generalitat Valenciana, permanent accommodation rooms and the event venue begin construction. Pool, garage, full perimeter fence in place.

2029

Camp Tariqa fully operational

Rural rooms open. Venue takes first event bookings — private dinners, retreats, weddings. The farm runs as a dual-revenue operation: citrus and hospitality, each feeding the other's story.

Glamping on the limestone terraces

Sleep where the oranges grow.

The terraced hillside at Finca Tariqa is one of the most distinctive agricultural landscapes in Valencia. The plan is to put people on it — in well-built, small-footprint structures that let the landscape do the work.

Coming 2027

Terrace tents

Canvas bell tents or similar demountable structures on the stone terraces. Set among the orange trees, looking across the valley. Each pitch is a cleared terrace with a level platform, a private outdoor space, and the hillside as the view.

Coming 2027

Shepherd huts

Small timber or steel-framed huts on wheels — insulated, with a wood burner for winter stays. Positioned on the upper terraces near the holm oak reforestation zone. Waking up to the forest and the grove below.

After DIC · 2029

Rural rooms

Four to eight permanent rooms in the restored farmhouse and new structures — registered as alojamiento rural under Valencian tourism law. For visitors who want more than a tent but the same view and the same farm.

The legal track: Demountable glamping structures can be authorised under Decreto 4/2024 as ancillary agro-tourism infrastructure — no land use change required. Permanent accommodation requires a Declaración de Interés Comunitario from the Generalitat Valenciana. We are pursuing both tracks simultaneously.

The People Behind It

First-generation. Post-DANA. A hillside nobody was watching.

Finca Tariqa was purchased in November 2025 by a woman under 40 with no inherited farming tradition. The first season was spent reading five years of cooperative records, commissioning a geology report, and learning what an 80-million-year-old hillside was trying to say.

Organic certification through CAECV is in preparation. The forest management plan is in preparation. Eco-tourism registration under Decreto 4/2024 is in preparation.
