Season 2026 · Call for builders

Before people can come to harvest,
someone has to build the camp.

We are preparing a basecamp for harvest volunteers — sleeping accommodation, a compost toilet block, and a shared working area on the hillside. We need people who know how to build. Or who want to learn.

Get in touch What we are building
The situation

The grove is ready.
The basecamp is not.

Finca Tariqa is a working citrus and reforestation farm on a limestone hillside outside Carcaixent, Valencia. From this harvest season we want to bring people to help pick the oranges and plant holm oaks — but we have nowhere to properly host them yet.

A small sleeping area, a composting toilet block, and a basecamp that can support a rotating group through harvest season.

We are looking for people who can help design and build this — in exchange for food, time on one of the most unusual agricultural landscapes in Spain, and the dwelling you build yourself.

01
Sleeping accommodation Simple, durable structures for two to four harvest volunteers — could be a converted outbuilding, timber frames, or a well-built tent platform. Needs to survive a Spanish winter.
02
Compost toilet block A properly designed composting toilet — not a hole in the ground. The right design serves the farm long-term and feeds the reforestation project. This is the most specific skill we need.
03
Basecamp working area Somewhere to store tools, gather before work, eat together. Shade, a table, storage. Permanent enough for years.
Who we are looking for

Builders. Learners. Both.

You do not need to be a professional. But you need to be serious about the work. This is a real build on a real farm, and what gets constructed will be used by people for years.

Specialists welcome

You know how to build

You have hands-on experience with construction, natural building, or sanitation systems. You can lead a build, problem-solve on site, and leave behind something that works. We will treat you as a collaborator, not a labourer.

  • Composting toilet design and installation
  • Timber framing or carpentry
  • Natural building — earth, lime, stone
  • Dry-stone walling or terrace repair
  • Permaculture design
  • Off-grid water and sanitation
No experience required

You want to learn

You have never built anything, but you want to. You are physically capable, you will follow instruction, and you are prepared to do the unglamorous work any real build involves. We will teach what we can.

  • Willing to work a full day outdoors
  • Not afraid of unfamiliar tools
  • Comfortable with ambiguity — plans change on site
  • Minimum two weeks — the learning curve is real
  • Interested in sustainable building, not just the trip
The exchange

What you get in return.

🏕️

You build where you sleep

The first structure you build is your own dwelling. You arrive with a tent and leave with something more permanent. That is the job.

🍊

Three meals a day

Cooked from the grove and the local market in Carcaixent. Spanish farmhouse food. No catering — real meals, eaten together.

🌿

The farm and the hillside

Eight thousand square metres of citrus, a limestone hillside under reforestation, and one of the oldest agricultural landscapes in Spain. Yours to explore when the work is done.
